# Business Recommendations

## Priority 1 – Implement Inventory Aging Management

**Problem:** Carry-over inventory continues to increase, while its contribution to completed listings remains relatively low.

**Recommendation:**

Implement an inventory classification system based on listing age.

**Classification 1 – New Listing (0–90 days)**
- Apply standard marketing activities.

**Classification 2 – Moderate (91–180 days)**
- Review leads and customer engagement.

**Classification 3 – Aging (181–365 days)**
- Conduct pricing and marketing reviews.

**Classification 4 – Old (366–730 days)**
- Reassess the property and relaunch the listing.

**Classification 5 – Stale (730+ days)**
- Revalidate the owner's interest or consider delisting.

**Actions:**
- Conduct monthly inventory reviews.
- Identify listings that have not generated any leads.
- Evaluate listing prices by comparing them with similar properties.
- Consider relisting only when there is a clear change in strategy.

## Priority 2 – Improve Listing Conversion Rate (Critical)

**Problem:** The listing conversion rate declined from 39% to 25%, falling below the 30% target.

**Actions:**
1. Identify listings that have not generated any leads within the last 30 days.
2. Evaluate listing quality, including photos, descriptions, pricing, and property information.
3. Audit duplicate listings associated with the same property.
4. Evaluate marketing channels based on completed conversions.
5. Focus on improving the conversion of existing listings before increasing inventory supply.

## Priority 3 – Develop Segment-Based Sales Strategies (Growth Opportunities)

**Problem:** Conversion performance and revenue contributions vary across property segments.

**Actions:**

- **Dhaka – Revenue Protection:** Prioritize high-quality inventory and high-value deals.
- **Barishal – Market Expansion:** Test gradual inventory expansion.
- **Rangpur – Performance Recovery:** Audit pricing, market demand, and inventory aging.
- **Apartment – Core Segment:** Maintain inventory supply and improve conversion performance.
- **Commercial – Conversion Improvement:** Evaluate property positioning and marketing channels.
- **House – Product Review:** Reassess pricing and property characteristics.
- **Cheap – Volume Strategy:** Maintain inventory in this segment due to its strong demand.
- **Expensive – Targeted Marketing:** Apply buyer segmentation and review pricing strategies.

## Priority 5 – Establish Inventory Acquisition Controls

**Problem:** Listing growth has not been matched by proportional growth in completed listings.

**Actions:**

Before accepting new listings, the company should evaluate the following factors:

- **Property Quality:** Assess the property's condition and overall marketability.
- **Competitive Pricing:** Compare listing prices with those of similar properties in the market.
- **Market Demand:** Evaluate demand for the relevant property type and location.
- **Existing Inventory:** Review existing inventory in the same location and property category.
- **Agent Capacity:** Assess the workload and capacity of agents managing the relevant property segment.
- **Carry-over Inventory Control:** Consider closing or delisting an existing carry-over listing before creating a new listing for the same property within the same year, where appropriate.
