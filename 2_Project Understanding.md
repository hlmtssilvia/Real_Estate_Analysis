# Project Understanding

## Project Overview

This project aims to provide data-driven information and insights to help the Sales Manager evaluate property and listing conversion performance and support business decision-making.

The analysis focuses on evaluating the effectiveness of converting listings and properties into successfully completed transactions, monitoring inventory absorption over time, and identifying factors and property segments associated with differences in conversion rates.

This project adopts an end-to-end data analytics approach, covering data preparation, database management, data analysis, dashboard development, and analytical result validation.

## Project Objective

To develop a **Real Estate Inventory Performance Dashboard** using Power BI to monitor property and listing conversion performance.

The dashboard is designed to provide the Sales Manager with a comprehensive overview of inventory conditions and performance, identify property segments with below-expectation conversion rates, and support data-driven decision-making to improve conversion performance and property inventory management efficiency.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Listing Conversion Rate:** Measures the proportion of listings successfully converted into completed transactions relative to the total number of listings.
- **Property Conversion Rate:** Measures the proportion of unique properties successfully sold relative to the total number of unique properties marketed through listings.
- **Completed Transactions:** Measures the total number of successfully completed property transactions.
- **Total Revenue:** Measures the total revenue generated from property transactions, based on the company's revenue definition.
- **Total Commission:** Measures the total commission earned by the company from successfully completed property transactions.

## Expected Business Value

- Monitor property and listing conversion performance against business targets.
- Evaluate inventory absorption and identify listings that have remained on the market for an extended period.
- Identify property segments with relatively higher or lower conversion performance.
- Evaluate the relationship between property characteristics, agent assignments, and conversion outcomes.
- Establish inventory management priorities and sales strategies based on transaction value and commission contributions.
